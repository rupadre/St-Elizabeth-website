A young mission with a long memory

Our church building has stood in Walbridge since 1883. Our community is older still in its own way.

  • 1936Bulgarian and Macedonian immigrants found St. George Parish in East Toledo.
  • 2003St. George Cathedral is consecrated in Rossford.
  • 2024The parish, now St. Elizabeth the New Martyr, celebrates its first Liturgy in Walbridge on Palm Sunday.
