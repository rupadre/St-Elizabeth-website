---
title: What are the Sacraments?
summary: The Sacraments, which we call the Holy Mysteries, are the ways Christ meets us and gives us His grace through ordinary things: water, oil, bread, wine and human touch.
order: 8
---
God created the material world and called it good. In Christ, He took on a human body. So it is natural that He heals and saves us through physical things, just as Jesus healed with mud and spittle and gave His Body and Blood at the Last Supper.

The main Mysteries are:

- **Baptism:** we die and rise with Christ and are born again.
- **Chrismation:** we receive the gift of the Holy Spirit, sealed with holy oil.
- **Holy Communion:** we receive the true Body and Blood of Christ.
- **Confession:** we are forgiven and restored.
- **Marriage:** a man and woman are united in Christ.
- **Holy Unction:** anointing for healing of soul and body.
- **Ordination:** bishops, priests and deacons are set apart to serve.

We call them "mysteries" not because they are puzzles, but because God works in them beyond what we can fully understand.

**Scripture:** John 3:5 · John 6:53–56 · James 5:14–16 · 1 John 1:9
