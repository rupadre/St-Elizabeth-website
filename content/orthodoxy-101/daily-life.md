---
title: How do Orthodox Christians live their faith day to day?
summary: Through prayer, fasting and almsgiving, the three practices Jesus taught in the Sermon on the Mount.
order: 9
---
**Prayer.** Many Orthodox Christians keep an icon corner at home, where they pray morning and evening. One of our best-loved prayers is the Jesus Prayer: "Lord Jesus Christ, Son of God, have mercy on me, a sinner." Repeated quietly through the day, it helps us "pray without ceasing."

**Fasting.** On most Wednesdays and Fridays, and in seasons such as Great Lent before Pascha (Easter), we set aside meat, dairy and some other foods. Fasting is not about dieting or earning God's favor. It trains us to say no to ourselves so we can say yes to God. It is always adapted to each person's health and circumstances, with a priest's guidance.

**Almsgiving.** We give to the poor and serve others, because in serving them we serve Christ.

These practices are not a burden laid on beginners. You grow into them gradually, with your priest's help.

**Scripture:** Matthew 6:1–18 · 1 Thessalonians 5:17 · Matthew 25:31–40
