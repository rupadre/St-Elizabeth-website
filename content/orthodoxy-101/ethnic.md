The Orthodox Church is a family of self-governing churches, such as those of Greece, Russia, Serbia, Romania and Bulgaria, all sharing the same faith and the same Communion. When immigrants came to America, they brought their churches with them, so you will find parishes with Greek, Russian or Bulgarian names. The name tells you about a parish's history, not who may join.

Our parish grew from St. George Parish, founded in East Toledo in 1936 by Bulgarian and Macedonian immigrants, and belongs to the Bulgarian Diocese, which Bulgarian and Macedonian communities formed in America. Since 1976 that diocese has been part of the Orthodox Church in America. We are grateful for our founders, who kept the faith alive in a new land.

Today, our services are almost entirely in English, and our parish is made up of people from many backgrounds: Orthodox from birth, former Protestants and Catholics, and people who came to faith for the first time. You do not need to learn a new language or culture to become Orthodox. You only need to come and follow Christ.
