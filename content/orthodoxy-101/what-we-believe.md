---
title: What do Orthodox Christians believe?
summary: We believe what the Nicene Creed confesses: one God in Trinity, and Jesus Christ, truly God and truly man, who died and rose for our salvation.
order: 2
---
The Creed was written by the whole Church at two great councils in AD 325 and 381. We sing it at every Divine Liturgy. In simple words, it says:

- **God the Father** made everything, seen and unseen.
- **Jesus Christ**, His eternal Son, became a man for us, was crucified, rose on the third day and will come again.
- **The Holy Spirit** is the Lord and Giver of life, worshipped together with the Father and the Son.
- **The Church** is one, holy, catholic and apostolic.
- **Baptism** washes away sin, and we look for the resurrection of the dead and the life to come.

If you are a Christian already, much of this will be familiar. We say the Creed exactly as it was first written, without later additions.

**Scripture:** Matthew 28:19 · John 1:1–14 · 1 Corinthians 15:3–4
