---
title: How does someone become Orthodox?
summary: You come, you learn, and with your priest's guidance you are received into the Church, a journey usually taking several months to a year.
order: 14
---
There is no pressure and no rush. Most people follow these steps:

1. **Visit.** Come to services several times. Watch, listen and pray as you are able.
2. **Talk with Fr. Arkady.** Tell him about your faith journey and your questions.
3. **Become an inquirer.** Attend church services regularly, pray to God, learn more about the faith, and grow in your decision to become Orthodox.
4. **Become a catechumen.** After a couple of months as an inquirer, once you are fairly certain you want to become Orthodox, talk to Fr. Arkady about being enrolled as a catechumen. At this point you may join our Zoom classes for catechumens. You are welcome to take full part in the life of the Church and the parish, except for Holy Communion and the other sacraments.
5. **Be received.** Through Baptism and Chrismation, or Chrismation alone if you were already baptized in the name of the Holy Trinity (Father, Son and Holy Spirit), you become a member of the Church and receive Holy Communion for the first time.

Your priest will guide each step and decide the timing with you. Along the way, you will be given a sponsor, a godparent, who walks with you.
