title: What is salvation?

Many Christians think of salvation mainly as forgiveness: God pardons our sins so we can go to heaven. Orthodox Christians believe that too, and much more. Sin is not only a broken rule; it is a sickness that wounds us and separates us from God. Christ came as the Physician of our souls and bodies.

God became man so that we might share in His life. St. Peter calls this becoming "partakers of the divine nature." The Church Fathers call it *theosis*: being filled with God's grace, the way iron placed in a fire glows with its heat.

This healing happens through God's grace and our willing response: in prayer, in the sacraments, in repentance and in love for our neighbor. It is not earned, and it is not a one-time event. It is a relationship that grows.

**Scripture:** 2 Peter 1:3–4 · Philippians 2:12–13 · Matthew 9:12
