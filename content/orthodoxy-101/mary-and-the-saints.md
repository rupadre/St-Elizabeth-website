---
title: Do Orthodox Christians worship Mary?
summary: No. We worship God alone, and we honor Mary as the Theotokos, the Mother of God, because she gave birth to Jesus Christ, who is God.

*Theotokos* is a Greek word meaning "God-bearer." The Church gave Mary this title in AD 431 to protect the truth about Jesus: the child she carried was not merely a man, but God in the flesh. Honoring her is a way of confessing who her Son is.

Mary herself said, "All generations will call me blessed." We do exactly that. We see in her the first and model Christian, the one who said yes to God completely: "Let it be to me according to your word."

When we ask Mary and the saints to pray for us, we are doing what you do when you ask a friend to pray for you. Those who have died in Christ are alive in Him, and "the prayer of a righteous person has great power." One family in heaven and on earth prays together.
