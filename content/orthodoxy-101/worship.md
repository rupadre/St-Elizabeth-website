The word *liturgy* means "the work of the people." It is not a performance to watch but a prayer we all offer. Christians worshipped this way from the beginning, as the Jews did in the Temple and synagogue before them.

You may notice several things:

- **Singing:** almost everything is sung, as the angels sing in heaven.
- **Incense:** a sign of our prayers rising to God.
- **Standing:** the ancient posture of prayer and resurrection. Seats are always available if you need them.
- **The Sign of the Cross and bowing:** we pray with our bodies as well as our minds.
- **Candles:** a sign that Christ is the Light of the world.

The Divine Liturgy is the Church's thanksgiving, climaxing in Holy Communion. It joins us to the worship of heaven described in the Book of Revelation.
