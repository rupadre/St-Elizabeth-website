In the New Testament, the Apostles laid hands on new leaders and appointed elders in every city. By the end of the first century, St. Ignatius of Antioch, a disciple of the Apostles, describes each local church gathered around its **bishop**, assisted by **priests** and **deacons**. This is called *apostolic succession*: a living connection, hand to hand, back to the Apostles.

The bishop guards the faith and unites his people. The priest serves a parish on his behalf, leading worship, teaching and caring for souls. Deacons assist in worship and service. Priests in our tradition may be married, if they marry before ordination.
