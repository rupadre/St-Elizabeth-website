---
title: What is the Orthodox Church?
summary: The Orthodox Church is the Church Jesus Christ founded, continuing from the Apostles to today in the same faith and worship.
order: 1
---
On the day of Pentecost, the Holy Spirit came upon the Apostles, and the Church was born in Jerusalem. From there the Apostles carried the Gospel across the world, ordaining bishops in every city. The Orthodox Church is that same community, still holding the faith they taught, still celebrating the worship they handed down.

Orthodox Christians are found all over the world: in Greece and Russia, in the Middle East, in Africa, and in towns like Walbridge, Ohio. Though our parishes speak many languages, we share one faith, one Communion and one life in Christ.

You may be surprised by how ancient our worship feels. That is because it is. When you join us on Sunday, you are praying with Christians of every century, using prayers many of which are more than 1,500 years old.

**Scripture:** Acts 2:1–42 · Matthew 16:18 · Ephesians 2:19–22
