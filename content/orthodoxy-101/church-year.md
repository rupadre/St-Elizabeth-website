The Church year begins on September 1. At its center is **Pascha** (Easter), the "Feast of Feasts," when we celebrate Christ's Resurrection. Around it are the **Twelve Great Feasts**, including the Nativity of Christ (Christmas), Theophany (His Baptism), the Transfiguration, Palm Sunday, the Ascension and Pentecost, along with several feasts of the Theotokos.

Feasts are prepared for by fasts. The longest is **Great Lent**, forty days before Holy Week. Others include the Nativity Fast before Christmas, which begins November 15.

Our parish celebrates Christmas on December 25. Pascha is calculated by the ancient method set by the early Church, so it often falls on a different Sunday from Western Easter.

Every day also remembers saints, so there is always someone to learn from and pray with.
