Many of our parishioners grew up in Baptist, Methodist, Evangelical, Lutheran and non-denominational churches. Most came because they were reading Scripture and church history and wanted to know what the first Christians believed and how they worshipped.

**What will feel familiar:** the Bible read aloud and sung in every service, faith in the Trinity and in Christ's death and resurrection, and a call to personal repentance and holiness.

**What may feel new:** liturgical worship, icons, honor given to Mary and the saints, and the sacraments at the center of Christian life. These are usually the questions people bring first, so we've answered them simply:

- [Do Orthodox Christians believe the Bible?](/orthodoxy-101/bible-and-tradition/)
- [Isn't kissing icons idolatry?](/orthodoxy-101/icons/)
- [Do Orthodox Christians worship Mary?](/orthodoxy-101/mary-and-the-saints/)
- [What is salvation?](/orthodoxy-101/salvation/)
- [Why is Orthodox worship so different?](/orthodoxy-101/worship/)
