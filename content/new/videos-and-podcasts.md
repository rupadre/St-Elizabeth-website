## Start here

- [What is Orthodox Christianity?](https://www.ancientfaith.com/podcasts/bethebee/what_is_orthodox_christianity), a short overview from *Be the Bee*
- [Six-Minute Church Tour](https://www.ancientfaith.com/podcasts/bethebee/six_minute_church_tour), what you'll see inside an Orthodox church

## Video series

- [*Welcome to the Orthodox Church*](https://discovertheearlychurch.org/video), Frederica Mathewes-Green. Short clips, one question each.
- [*Journey to Fullness*](https://frbarnabaspowell.substack.com/p/a-journey-to-fullness), Fr. Barnabas Powell. A 16-part series for seekers, with a workbook.

## Podcasts from Ancient Faith

- [*Orthodoxy and Heterodoxy*](https://www.ancientfaith.com/podcasts/orthodoxyheterodoxy), Fr. Andrew Stephen Damick. How Orthodoxy compares with Catholic, Protestant and other beliefs.
- [*Our Life in Christ*](https://app.ancientfaith.com/podcasts/ourlife/), Steve Robinson and Bill Gould. Especially helpful for evangelicals.
- [*How to Be an Orthodox Christian*](https://app.ancientfaith.com/podcasts/how-to-be-an-orthodox-christian/), Dcn. Seraphim Rohlin. Lessons used to teach catechumens.
- [*Orthodoxy Live*](https://app.ancientfaith.com/podcasts/orthodoxylive/), Fr. Evan Armatas. A live call-in show for questions, Sundays at 8 PM Eastern.
- *An Introduction to God*, Fr. Andrew Stephen Damick, for those new to faith ([listen](https://roadsfromemmaus.org/podcasts/))
