Some people come to us after years without any faith. Some come from other religions. Some are simply drawn by the beauty of the services, or by a feeling that there must be more to life. All are welcome.

Christianity is not first a set of rules or ideas. It is a relationship with a Person, Jesus Christ, who Christians believe is God become man. The Orthodox Church has kept the faith and worship of the first Christians for 2,000 years, and invites everyone to "come and see."

You don't need to know when to stand or what to say. Watch, listen and ask anything. Nothing about a visit commits you to anything.
