Pascha, the feast of Christ's Resurrection, is the center of the Orthodox year. In 2024 it fell on May 5, just one week after the parish served its first Liturgy in Walbridge.

At midnight the parish went out of the church in procession by candlelight and sang "Christ is risen from the dead" at the church doors, then celebrated the Paschal Liturgy together.
