---
title: Glossary
summary: Plain meanings for words you'll hear at an Orthodox church.
---
**Antidoron.** Blessed bread given to everyone at the end of the Divine Liturgy, including visitors.

**Archbishop, bishop.** A successor of the Apostles who oversees a diocese. Ours is Archbishop Alexander.

**Catechumen.** Someone formally preparing to be received into the Orthodox Church.

**Chrismation.** Anointing with holy oil that seals a person with the gift of the Holy Spirit; it follows Baptism.

**Divine Liturgy.** The main Sunday service, in which Holy Communion is celebrated.

**Feast.** A day celebrating an event in the life of Christ or the Theotokos, or a saint.

**Great Vespers.** The Saturday evening service that begins the celebration of Sunday.

**Hours.** Short services of psalms and prayers, sung before the Sunday Liturgy.

**Icon.** A holy image of Christ, the Theotokos or a saint, honored as a window to the person shown.

**Iconostasis.** The wall of icons between the nave and the altar.

**Inquirer.** Anyone learning about the Orthodox faith, with no commitment required.

**Jesus Prayer.** A short prayer repeated quietly: "Lord Jesus Christ, Son of God, have mercy on me."

**Narthex.** The entrance area of the church.

**Nave.** The main part of the church where the people stand and pray.

**Pascha.** Easter, the feast of Christ's Resurrection.

**Theotokos.** "God-bearer," the Church's title for the Virgin Mary, Mother of God.

**Tea for the Theotokos.** A parish gathering held around feasts of the Theotokos.

**Vespers.** Evening prayer, served on Saturdays and the eves of feasts.

**Vigil.** Vespers and Matins served together on the eve of a great feast.
