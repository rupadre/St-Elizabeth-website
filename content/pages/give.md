St. Elizabeth's is a young mission in a building more than 140 years old. Every gift, large or small, helps care for the church, support our clergy and share the Orthodox faith in Walbridge and Toledo.

[Give online through Tithe.ly](https://tithe.ly/give/?c=1516171){: .button }

You can also give in person during any service.

Visiting for the first time? Please don't feel any obligation to give. We are simply glad you came.
