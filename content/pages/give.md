St. Elizabeth's is a young mission in a building more than 140 years old. Every gift, large or small, helps care for the church, support our clergy and share the Orthodox faith in Walbridge and Toledo.

[Give online through Tithe.ly](https://tithe.ly/give/?c=1516171){: .button }

Visiting for the first time? Please don't feel any obligation to give. We are simply glad you came.

## Other ways to give

- **In person.** Cash or a check in the collection basket during any service.
- **Credit card at church.** You can also give by card in person at church.
- **Online, any time.** Through [Tithe.ly](https://tithe.ly/give/?c=1516171), once or as a regular gift.

## Where your gifts go

- **Operating Fund.** All tithes go here. It pays the ongoing costs of keeping our church open: salaries, utilities and office expenses.
- **Church Renovations Fund.** For the care and repair of our 1883 church building.
- **Alms Fund.** Help for people in need.
- **Priest's Discretionary Fund.**
- **Special Purpose Funds.** For particular needs and projects as they arise.

## Make a pledge

A responsibility and concern for that with which we have been entrusted is an integral part of the lives of all Orthodox Christians. All that we have, our talents, material possessions, health, are given to us by God and we seek simply to give back to Him according to our means. Our attitude toward God, our love and gratitude to Him for what we have, is what's important in our offering to Him.

Please take a moment to reflect on your blessings and decide for yourself how much you are able to return to God in support of His Church. Some parishioners set the amount of their donations equal to the Biblical level of 10% of income. Each person's contribution is determined by his current material situation. Please do your best at fulfilling your annual offering commitment. The expenses of maintaining His home (salaries, utilities, office expenses) are ongoing, just as in any earthly organization, and regular contributions are necessary to meet them. May the Lord bless you!
