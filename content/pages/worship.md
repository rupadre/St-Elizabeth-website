## The services

**Divine Liturgy.** The heart of Orthodox worship and the oldest form of Christian worship still in use. We hear Scripture, pray for the world and celebrate Holy Communion. About 90 minutes.

**Hours.** Short morning psalms and prayers sung just before the Sunday Liturgy.

**Great Vespers.** Evening prayer on Saturday that begins the celebration of Sunday, with psalms, hymns and candlelight. Shorter and quieter, and a good first service for visitors.

**Vigil.** On the eve of great feasts, Vespers and the morning service of Matins are served together.

Not sure what something means? See our [glossary](/glossary/), or ask anyone after the service.
